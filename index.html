Connect

1. Plug the S-1 straight into the phone with a USB-C cable.

2. On the S-1, turn AIRA Link off (menu item "A.LnK"), then power it off and on.

3. Tap Connect MIDI and allow access. After that, MIDI reconnects on its own.

4. Turn knobs and press pads. The values below follow what the S-1 sends.

Sound Match

Play a sound for 5 seconds (from Spotify, YouTube or anything else) near the phone's microphone, or pick an audio file. The app listens and suggests how to build it on the S-1.

Ready

1. Phone apps cannot share their audio with a web page, so let the sound play out loud near the phone and start listening just before the part you want.

2. Best results: a single instrument or drum hit in a quiet room. A full mix has many sounds at once, so the guess will be rough.

3. Stop the scope on the Sound tab first, so the microphone is free.

The S-1 cannot send its stored pattern over MIDI. These views build up from the clock, notes and pattern changes it sends while it plays, so they fill in during the first pass. To see pattern numbers, turn "txPc" on in the S-1 menu and set the S-1 sync to internal ("SYnC" then "Int") so it sends clock.

Steps

Lit steps are where the S-1 played a note in the last two passes, labelled with the lowest note. The outlined step is the playhead.

Piano roll

Bright notes are the current pass and faded notes the pass before. Harder velocity is brighter. The S-1 does not send Note Off, so lengths follow the Note length setting.
